# Prompt cards

Copy one into Codex and change the parts in brackets. Codex already knows the Soupop rules, so you don't need to explain tables.

## Quick answers

1. How many confirmed invoices did we have [last week], and how does that compare with the week before?
2. What is the total scheduled BNPL repayment due in [the next 30 days], excluding the first installment?
3. How many installments are overdue today, and what is the overdue amount by product?
4. How many people used the chatbot [yesterday], in Tehran time, and how many chats did they send?
5. Where are open loan applications stuck right now? Count them by step.

## Saved questions

6. Show daily confirmed sales for [the last 90 days] as a line chart and save it as a question.
7. Make a question showing the share of installments paid on time by month, and save it.

## Dashboards

8. Build a dashboard "[Weekly sales overview]" with confirmed sales, number of invoices, average invoice value, and a daily sales line, with a date filter.
9. Build a dashboard for the collections team: amount due this week, overdue amount, overdue count by days late, and collected cash by day.
10. Build a chatbot usage dashboard: chats per day, active users per day, and top question tags, with a date filter.

## Tips

- Say the period and whether you mean Tehran days.
- Ask "how did you calculate this?" whenever a number surprises you.
- New questions and dashboards go in the Staff collection. To change an existing dashboard, ask an analyst.

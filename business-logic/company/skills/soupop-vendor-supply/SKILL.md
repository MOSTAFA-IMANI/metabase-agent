# SouPop Vendor Supply

## Purpose

Use this skill for vendor-side operations, marketplace supply, business development, and vendor growth.

## Vendor definition

A SouPop vendor is an automotive service provider contracted with SouPop that provides automotive services to customers.

## Vendor journey

1. **Acquisition:** Identify and recruit automotive service providers.
2. **Contracting:** Establish a contractual relationship with SouPop.
3. **Onboarding:** Bring the vendor into the SouPop network.
4. **Activation:** Enable the vendor to accept customers using SouPop credit.
5. **Transaction growth:** Increase the number and value of successful transactions.
6. **Retention:** Maintain ongoing vendor participation and activity.

## Analysis guidelines

When analyzing vendor supply:

- Distinguish recruited, contracted, onboarded, activated, and actively transacting vendors.
- Do not assume that signing a contract means a vendor is operationally active.
- Consider vendor activity alongside customer demand and credit availability.
- Examine both transaction count and transaction value.
- Identify potential supply constraints that prevent customers from completing transactions.

## Useful questions

- How many contracted vendors are actively accepting SouPop credit?
- What proportion of onboarded vendors complete transactions?
- Which vendors have stopped transacting?
- What prevents vendors from accepting credit-funded purchases?
- How do vendor activity and transaction value change over time?

## Guardrails

The source does not specify vendor categories, geographic coverage, onboarding duration, commission rates, vendor performance, or vendor-level targets. Request verified information before presenting these as facts.

# SouPop Customer Demand

## Purpose

Use this skill for customer-side product, marketing, funnel, and growth questions.

## Customer definition

A SouPop customer is a car owner who can use SouPop credit to obtain automotive services from a contracted vendor.

## Customer journey

1. **Acquisition:** Attract car owners to SouPop.
2. **Registration and activation:** Encourage sign-up and meaningful product use.
3. **Credit activation and usage:** Help eligible customers access and use BNPL credit or a loan.
4. **Transaction:** The customer uses SouPop credit to purchase a service from a contracted vendor.
5. **Retention:** Encourage repeat visits and purchases.

Features such as service records, service reminders, and vehicle management may support acquisition, engagement, and retention. They complement the financial and transaction core.

## Analysis guidelines

When analyzing customer growth:

- Separate acquisition, registration, activation, credit usage, first transaction, repeat transaction, and retention.
- Do not treat registered users as equivalent to active transacting customers.
- Do not treat allocated credit as revenue.
- Consider vendor availability and service coverage as dependencies for customer conversion.
- Identify missing funnel definitions and measurements before calculating conversion rates.

## Useful questions

- Where do customers drop off before their first transaction?
- What proportion of registered customers use credit?
- What prevents a customer from completing a transaction?
- How often do customers return for another service?
- Which non-financial features support engagement or retention?

## Guardrails

The source does not provide actual conversion rates, acquisition costs, retention rates, customer segments, or campaign performance. Do not fabricate these values. Request data or label proposed metrics as recommendations.

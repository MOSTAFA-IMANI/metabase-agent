# SouPop Business Overview

## Purpose

Use this skill to explain SouPop's business model consistently and distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions.

## Business model

SouPop is an automotive fintech platform. It connects car owners who need automotive services, access to BNPL credit or loans, and contracted automotive service vendors.

Customers use SouPop credit or a loan to obtain services from a contracted vendor. The transaction is recorded, an invoice is issued, settlement follows the applicable contractual mechanism, and SouPop earns commission from the vendor.

## Roles

- **Customer:** A car owner who uses SouPop credit to obtain automotive services.
- **Vendor:** A contracted automotive service provider that delivers services to customers.
- **SouPop:** The platform connecting customers, credit, and vendors and earning commission on completed transactions.

## Core workflow

1. The customer accesses BNPL credit or a loan through SouPop.
2. The customer visits a contracted automotive service vendor.
3. The vendor provides the required automotive service.
4. The transaction is recorded and an invoice is issued.
5. Settlement follows the relevant contract, and SouPop earns vendor commission.

## Business areas

- **Customer demand:** Acquisition, registration, activation, credit usage, successful transactions, and retention.
- **Vendor supply:** Vendor acquisition, contracting, onboarding, activation, transaction growth, and retention.
- **Transaction core:** Connecting customer credit to vendor services, recording purchases, invoicing, settlement, and commission revenue.

Customer and vendor acquisition are separate processes that meet when a transaction occurs.

## Revenue and success

The stated primary revenue model is commission charged to vendors on transactions. User count and allocated credit alone are not revenue. Successful transaction count, transaction value, and commission revenue are important commercial outcomes.

- Distinguish allocated credit, transaction value, and revenue.
- Label recommendations and hypotheses as suggestions, not established SouPop facts.

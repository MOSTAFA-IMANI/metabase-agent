# SouPop Growth and Metrics

## Purpose

Use this skill to organize SouPop growth planning and business reviews around the platform's two-sided marketplace and transaction-based revenue model.

## Core principle

SouPop's sustainable growth depends on both sides of the network:

- Customers must be acquired, activated, and motivated to use credit for automotive services.
- Vendors must be contracted, activated, and able to serve customers.
- The two sides meet when customers complete transactions with contracted vendors.

## High-level funnel

### Customer side

1. Acquisition
2. Registration and engagement
3. Credit activation and usage
4. Successful transaction
5. Repeat transaction and retention

### Vendor side

1. Vendor acquisition
2. Contracting
3. Onboarding
4. Activation
5. Successful transactions
6. Ongoing activity and retention

### Transaction and revenue outcomes

- Successful transaction count
- Transaction value
- Vendor commission revenue

## Suggested measurement framework

The source establishes business stages and outcomes but does not provide a complete metric dictionary. The following are suggested measurements, not confirmed existing SouPop metrics:

| Area | Potential measurement |
|---|---|
| Customer acquisition | New customer registrations |
| Customer activation | Customers reaching a defined activation event |
| Credit usage | Customers who use available credit |
| Customer conversion | Customers completing a first successful transaction |
| Retention | Customers completing repeat transactions within a defined period |
| Vendor supply | Contracted and activated vendor counts |
| Vendor activity | Vendors completing transactions within a defined period |
| Transactions | Successful transaction count |
| Transaction value | Total value of successful transactions |
| Revenue | Commission revenue recognized under the applicable financial rules |

## Review guidelines

When preparing a growth review:

1. State the period and metric definitions.
2. Separate actual results from targets and forecasts.
3. Analyze customer and vendor performance together.
4. Identify where the funnel is losing customers or vendors.
5. Connect transaction growth to transaction value and commission revenue.
6. Identify missing data and propose specific next steps.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent historical results, targets, growth rates, conversion rates, or revenue.
- Do not treat user count or allocated credit as sufficient evidence of business success.
- Do not calculate commission revenue without a confirmed commission rate and applicable rules.
- Clearly label proposed metrics, hypotheses, and recommendations.
